# QuoteMuse — Image Guide

## Concept
AI Quote Generator — writes original, on-brand inspirational quotes and turns them into
branded quote cards ready for social media. Light theme with indigo (`#4f46e5`) + gold
(`#d97706`) accents and an editorial, Playfair Display feel.

## Regeneration Prompts
Keep **zero readable text** in every image. Light backgrounds, indigo `#4f46e5` + gold
`#d97706` accents, editorial/premium mood.

1. **Hero:** Sunlit minimalist desk, laptop screen showing a glowing abstract indigo/gold quote card with flowing calligraphic swirl lines, small plant, coffee cup, soft bokeh, no letters
2. **AI Writing:** Glowing elegant feather quill made of light with indigo/gold particle trails forming abstract wavy lines, no readable words
3. **Brand Voice:** Flat lay of indigo/gold color swatches, blank typography specimen blocks, and a paint brush on a light surface
4. **Card Templates:** Grid of blank elegant quote card mockups in different aspect ratios with gold flourish borders, no text
5. **Hashtags:** Abstract cluster of glowing indigo/gold hashtag symbols floating in soft light, airy background
6. **Batch Packs:** Stack of blank indigo/gold quote cards fanned out like a deck of cards, soft shadows
7. **One-Click Export:** Laptop and phone both displaying matching abstract indigo/gold quote card designs, light bright background

## Notes
- All images are local under `public/images/`, compressed as JPEG at quality 82
- No marketing copy, headings, or taglines baked into any image
- Every `<img>` has descriptive alt text
- Run `npm run check-images` to verify all required files are present
